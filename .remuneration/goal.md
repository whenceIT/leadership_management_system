WHENCE FINANCIAL SERVICES MANAGERIAL REMUNERATION GUIDE SUMMARY


EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Whence Financial Services is on a trajectory to reach a $100 million valuation within five years from 2026. This growth will not come from branch expansion alone, but from enhanced operational efficiency, improved portfolio quality, innovation, and sustainable leadership.
This proposal reimagines managerial remuneration as a strategic lever for value creation, not just a cost. It shifts from a scattered, entitlement-based approach to a performance-aligned, data-driven framework where rewards are tied directly to:
•	Revenue protection and growth
•	Risk reduction and default management
•	Client retention and product innovation
•	Operational efficiency and staff development
Managers will be rewarded not for tenure, but for tangible contributions to institutional resilience and growth.
TIER STRUCTURE (APPLICABLE TO ALL MANAGERIAL ROLES)
Tier	Seniority Level	Revenue / Responsibility Level
Tier 1	Most Senior	Highest revenue responsibility
Tier 2	Senior	High revenue responsibility
Tier 3	Mid-Level	Medium revenue responsibility
Tier 4	Junior	Entry managerial responsibility (Those in acting capacity  or Coordinators)
 Revenue scale and institutional responsibility take precedence over branch count.
GUIDING ASSUMPTIONS (Whence Financial Services Remuneration Structure)
1)	Gross Pay: This is the cash salary paid to the employee. It is taxable and subject to statutory deductions (PAYE, NAPSA, NHIMA). It includes base salary plus any cash allowances (e.g., transport, fuel).
2)	Net Pay (Cash): This is the cash amount the employee receives in their bank account after all statutory deductions (PAYE, NAPSA, NHIMA) have been applied to Gross Pay.
3)	Housing Allowance: This is a non-taxable benefit in kind because the institution pays it directly to the landlord. It is not included in Gross Pay but is very much a cost to the company and considered part of the employee's total compensation package.
4)	Total Monthly Cost to Company: This is the full cash outflow from the institution for that employee for one month. It is calculated as:
1.	Gross Pay (cash to employee)
2.	+ Employer statutory contributions (NAPSA, NHIMA)
3.	+ Employer private pension contribution (e.g, Saturnia)
4.	+ Housing Allowance (paid directly to landlord)
SAMPLE CALCULATION (Branch Manager - Band 2)
Using the Branch Manager example to illustrate how this looks:
Item	Amount (ZMW)	Notes
Gross Pay	19,948	Cash salary (taxable). This is the figure on the employment contract.
Less: Statutory Deductions (PAYE, NAPSA, NHIMA)	(6,947)	Approximate deductions.
Net Pay (Cash to Employee)	13,001	Cash deposited into employee's bank account.
		
Housing Allowance (Paid to Landlord)	+2,500	Non-taxable benefit in kind (Band 2). Not part of Gross Pay.
Employer Statutory & Pension Costs	+3,285	Employer NAPSA, NHIMA, and Saturnia contributions.
Total Monthly Cost to Company	25,733	Gross Pay + Employer Costs + Housing Payment
So in this example, the employee receives K13,001 in cash + K2,500 in rent paid = K15,501 in total direct value, while the company's total cash outflow is K25,733.
UNDERSTANDING THE REMUNERATION FIGURES
Term	Definition	Tax Status
Gross Pay	The employee's monthly cash salary. This is the base amount before statutory deductions (PAYE, NAPSA, NHIMA). It increases with tier level.	Taxable (PAYE)
Net Pay (Cash)	The actual cash amount deposited into the employee's bank account after all statutory deductions.	N/A
Housing Allowance	A non-taxable benefit where the institution pays rent directly to the landlord. Amount increases with tier performance.	Non-Taxable
Total Monthly Cost to Company	The full cash outflow from the institution: Gross Pay + Employer Statutory Contributions (NAPSA, NHIMA) + Employer Pension (Saturnia) + Housing Allowance.	N/A

5. Key Rules on Increments
•	Conditional: Salary increments are not automatic. They are tied to achieving the KPIs listed above.
•	Revocable: If performance drops, the process is:
1.	Month 1: Manager must admit failure and submit a corrective plan.
2.	Months 2 & 3: Monthly progress reports are required if performance is still below threshold.
3.	Post Month 3: If no improvement, the Administration Department can suspend, adjust, or revoke the increment.
•	Monitoring: Performance is reviewed monthly and must be verified by internal audits.
•	All prior increment policy terms previously approved by management specific to the roles included in this document shall become null and void upon approval of this framework.

b. DISTRICT MANAGERS
- District Managers shall earn a performance allowance of K8000 if they attain an 80% Branch Efficiency and PDUA for that particular year.
ALLOWANCES
All previously approved allowances granted outside the previously increment policy framework, including but not limited to travel allowance, funeral allowance, housing allowance, and settlement allowance, shall remain valid, accessible, and enforceable under the same approved terms and conditions unless expressly revised or withdrawn through a formal management resolution
Pension & Medical
•	Pension (Saturnia): A voluntary, additional pension scheme is proposed. Suggestion: Employee contributes 5%, Employer contributes 7%.
•	Medical Scheme (OneLife): Proposed for Top Management initially. Minimum premium: K658/month for the principal member.
